# Write an effective brief

> **TL;DR** A good brief names the **outcome**, **audience**, **sources**, **limits**, **deliverables** and **how you will judge it**.

## Steps

1. **Outcome**: what you want.
2. **Audience**: who it is for.
3. **Source material**: files and facts to use.
4. **Constraints**: firm rules first, wishes after.
5. **Required states or deliverables**.
6. **Acceptance criteria**: how you will check it.
7. Say what is **out of scope**.

## Example

> Create a responsive product page for engineering managers. Use the attached brand guide and approved product facts. Include loading and error states for the signup form. Keep account administration out of scope. The page passes when the mobile layout has no overflow and the form reports success and failure clearly.

## If something goes wrong

- Agent asks a **broad question**? Add the missing decision or evidence.
- Rules **conflict**? Pick the one that wins. Do not add more vague text.

## Good to know

- A detailed brief still does **not** promise a fixed result.
- Check generated **facts, links** and key **workflows**.
