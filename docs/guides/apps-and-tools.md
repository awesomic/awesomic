# Apps and tools

> **TL;DR** **Connected apps** and **tools** let tasks use outside services. Each keeps its own permissions.

![Connect agents and add your own MCP server](../../.github/assets/screenshots/connect-agents.jpg)

## Steps

1. Pick the app you need.
2. Review the **permissions** it asks for.
3. Connect it. Finish any **secure browser sign-in** step.
4. Go back to your task. The tools are now available to supported tasks.

## What you get

- The task uses the **approved connection** for your workflow.
- It tells you when **access is missing**.

## If something goes wrong

- Check the **connection status** and the permission asked for.
- Reconnect **only** through the settings flow. Then return to your task.

## Good to know

- **Never paste credentials** into a task or docs.
- A connected app does **not** allow unrelated data access or actions.
