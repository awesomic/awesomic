# Plans and credits

> **TL;DR** Your **plan**, **personal credits** and **team budget** decide which models and actions you can use.

![Settings, Usage](../../.github/assets/screenshots/usage.jpg)

## Steps

1. Check you are on the **right account** and **team**.
2. Look at the **model selector** and any **billing message**.
3. If access is missing, do the **account action** Awesomic shows.
4. Refresh the billing state after adding credits or changing plan.
5. Go back to your task. Confirm the model. Press **Send**.

## How it works

- Awesomic checks the **signed-in account** and **selected team**.
- A **plan** can give access.
- **Personal credits** can pay for eligible work.
- A **team** can have its own **budget**.

## Good to know

- Access can differ by **account, team, model and action**.
- A docs page does **not** promise every account can use a feature.
- For prices, see [awesomic.com](https://awesomic.com).
