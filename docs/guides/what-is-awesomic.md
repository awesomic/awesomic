# What is Awesomic?

> **TL;DR** Awesomic is a **project workspace**: you describe the outcome, an agent works on it in chat, you review and refine.

![The Awesomic home screen with the prompt box](../../.github/assets/screenshots/recents-header.jpg)

## Steps

1. Open an **editable project**.
2. Describe the outcome in the **prompt box**.
3. Press **Send** and follow progress in the **task chat**.
4. Review the **files** or **previews** the task makes.
5. Send **follow-up messages** to fix, change or extend the work.

## What you get

- The request, progress, chat and outputs stay **together in one task**.
- Outputs depend on the request: **files, previews, both, or neither**.

## If something goes wrong

- Project selector says **Loading**? Keep writing and wait.
- It says **No projects**? Open or create an editable project first.
- **Send** is greyed out? Add a request. Wait for uploads, imports or voice input to finish.
- You see an **error**? Follow the next step it names.
- A **billing** message means you need an active plan, personal credits or team budget.

## Good to know

- Awesomic is a **general workspace**, not only a landing-page tool.
