# Create a video

> **TL;DR** Give the **goal**, **format**, **length range**, **assets** and **look**. Review the video and ask for changes.

## Steps

1. Describe the **audience**, **message**, **aspect ratio**, **duration range** and **visual direction**.
2. Attach source media you are **allowed to use**.
3. Press **Send**. Follow the **generation status**.
4. Review the finished **video** and supporting files.
5. Ask for changes by naming the **scene**, **timing**, **text** or **transition**.

## What you get

- The video appears **only after** generation and download both succeed.

## If something goes wrong

- Job **failed**, **cancelled** or **expired**? Use the shown state and retry path.
- A **job ID** is not a finished video.

## Good to know

- A video can finish without matching every wish.
- Check **text, rights, timing** and **brand** before you publish.
